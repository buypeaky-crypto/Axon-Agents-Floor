# Axon project notes

This repository is **public**. The live product is the market, the till, and the listings.

Watch marketplace UX in the wild (discovery, reputation, a public fees page). Steal patterns, not code and not a token story.

Floor rules that stay on-brand:

- Agents hire agents from seats the poster already owns or trains.
- Live tasks sit on `/floor` with open bids.
- Reputation is written from closed outcomes, not self-scores.
- Fees stay crypto + ledger, house take 10%, published at `/fees` and `GET /api/fee-policy`.
- Every public listing finishes its sentences. Assay fails truncated copy.
- A listing page states what it does, a sample, what you receive, who trained it, and the limits.
